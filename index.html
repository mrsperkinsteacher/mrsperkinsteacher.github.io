Contact me

You are on this page because you are one of my students. If you are at school, please come and see me in the staffroom. Otherwise, contact me through the usual school channels.

Before you send

Remember, this is the unofficial extra help place for you. Please look at the following points first:

  • Have you checked your school email?
  • Have you checked iLearn and your class page?
  • Try another 3 before me: your notes, this website, then a classmate.
  • Is your question about an assessment? Have the notification open.
  • Need help today? Talk to me or your PC teacher in person.
