Who to talk to

you are never on your own

Not sure where to start? Any of these people can help or point you to the right person:

  • Your class teacher
  • Your PC teacher
  • Your Year Coordinator
  • Student Support
  • Student Reception
Need to talk to someone right now? Kids Helpline is free and open 24/7 for young people: call 1800 55 1800 or visit kidshelpline.com.au.
